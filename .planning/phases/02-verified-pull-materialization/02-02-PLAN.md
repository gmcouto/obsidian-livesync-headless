## Phase Goal

**As a** vault operator, **I want to** dry-run and then materialize verified LiveSync remote files into an empty or dedicated vault, **so that** I can recover vault content without granting any remote write capability and without destroying unproven local or remote data.

<objective>
Expand the tracer decoder to encrypted V1/V2 documents, obfuscated paths, chunked plain/newnote assemblies, and fail-closed validation blockers.

Purpose: Real LiveSync vaults are encrypted, chunked, and often path-obfuscated; the tracer's inline notes body is not enough.
Output: Characterization-proven decode adapter and pull-plan blockers for COMP-02 through COMP-05.
</objective>
