## Phase Goal

**As a** vault operator, **I want to** dry-run and then materialize verified LiveSync remote files into an empty or dedicated vault, **so that** I can recover vault content without granting any remote write capability and without destroying unproven local or remote data.

<objective>
Prove all-leaf conflict detection, path safety, and empty-or-dedicated vault preflight so pull fails closed instead of writing a partial or colliding vault.

Purpose: COMP-06 and PULL-07 are the safety core of materialization; dedicated/empty is the PULL-02 gate.
Output: Conflict exit 8 with no winner file; path-policy and vault-preflight tests; vault.dedicated in Zod.
</objective>
